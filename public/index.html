Mathematical Capital Rationing Model Formulation

Formulating public infrastructure resource allocation as a Multi-Dimensional 0-1 Integer Linear Program (0-1 ILP) to maximize Social Return on Investment under fiscal and environmental constraints.

1. Binary Decision Variables

Because large municipal assets (Command Centres, Water Treatment Plants, Bus Rapid Transit) cannot be built fractionally and still deliver full public utility, we define binary decision variables $x_i \in \{0, 1\}$ for each candidate project $i \in \{1, \dots, n\}$:

$$x_i \in \{0, 1\} \quad \text{where } x_i = 1 \text{ if funded, } 0 \text{ otherwise}$$

2. Objective Function: Maximize Net Social Welfare

Let $c_i$ be the capital outlay (₹ Crores) and $\text{SROI}_i$ be the composite Social Rate of Interest percentage (capturing economic time savings, public health, and avoided damages). Net social value generated by project $i$ is $s_i = c_i \cdot \left(\frac{\text{SROI}_i}{10}\right)$.

$$\text{Maximize } Z = \sum_{i=1}^n s_i \cdot x_i = \sum_{i=1}^n \left(c_i \cdot \frac{\text{SROI}_i}{10}\right) \cdot x_i$$

3. Resource, Environmental & Structural Constraints

A. Capital Ceiling ($B_{\text{capex}}$): Total upfront outlay cannot exceed corpus.
$$\sum_{i=1}^n c_i x_i \le B_{\text{capex}}$$
B. Annual OPEX Cap ($B_{\text{opex}}$): Prevents recurring municipal insolvency.
$$\sum_{i=1}^n o_i x_i \le B_{\text{opex}}$$
C. Climate & Citizen Mandates: Minimum statutory impact floors.
$$\sum e_i x_i \ge C_{\min}, \quad \sum p_i x_i \ge P_{\min}$$
D. Digital Backbone Coupling: ITMS & Flood Grid require Command Center.
$$x_5 - x_1 \le 0, \quad x_{10} - x_1 \le 0$$

Why Greedy SROI Ranking Fails (Operations Research Proof)

A naive approach commonly found in public budgeting is ranking projects by highest benefit-cost ratio ($\text{SROI}_i$) and funding greedily. In Integer Programming, this fails for three structural reasons:

  1. Indivisibility ("Lumpy" Capital Outlays): High-ratio projects leave stranded budget slack that cannot fit other candidates, whereas complementary mid-sized projects fully exhaust the corpus for higher net return.
  2. Network Backbone Contingencies ($x_j \le x_k$): Core assets like Command Centres have moderate standalone SROI, but enable high-value downstream systems. Greedy ranking rejects or delays the backbone.
  3. Multi-Dimensional Constraints: Single-ratio ranking cannot concurrently balance capital expenditure, annual operations ceilings, and statutory carbon floors.
